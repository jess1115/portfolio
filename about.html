About Me

I recently completed an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Bristol, after completing my bachelor's degree in Advertising and Public Relations at Fu Jen Catholic University.

Before moving into computer science, I gained experience in communication, visual projects, service design, and project coordination. These experiences gave me an interest in how people interact with products and technology, and how ideas can be turned into practical outcomes.

During my MSc, I explored different areas of computer science through coursework and hands-on projects. I particularly enjoyed software development and working on projects where I could build, test, and improve something from the ground up.

I enjoy learning by building things and solving problems along the way. I'm particularly interested in software engineering and hope to continue developing my technical skills while working on practical software that people can use.
